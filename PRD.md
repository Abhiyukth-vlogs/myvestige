# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Vestige Redesign & E-Commerce Platform
**Project Name:** Vestige Next-Gen Platform ("A New Vestige")  
**Platform Reference:** [myvestige.com](https://www.myvestige.com/)  
**Version:** 2.0.0 (Modern Architecture & UI/UX Redesign)  
**Status:** Active Execution  

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CRITICAL CONSTRAINT:** The original logo, core products, core reviews, legacy data, and the fundamental structure of the website must remain **EXACTLY the same**. This project is strictly a visual and UI redesign to modernize the look and feel. Do not alter, remove, or modify any core items, brand assets, or existing content architecture.

---

## 1. Project Overview & Business Context

### 1.1 Business Model
Vestige Marketing Pvt. Ltd. operates as a leading direct-selling (MLM) enterprise established in 2004, offering world-class Health & Wellness, Personal Care, Cosmetics, and Agricultural products. The core motto is **"Wish You Wellth"** — spreading wealth through wellness and empowering individuals to achieve economic independence.

### 1.2 Target Audience
1. **Retail Customers:** General consumers seeking premium health supplements, organic personal care, Ayurvedic remedies, and eco-friendly home care products.
2. **Registered Distributors:** Active network marketing entrepreneurs tracking Point Value (PV), Business Volume (BV), downline hierarchies, bonus vouchers, training schedules (CNT), and achievement levels.
3. **Prospects:** Potential distributors evaluating the Vestige business opportunity, Car Fund, House Fund, and Travel Fund programs.

### 1.3 Global Presence
- **Active Operational Markets:** India, UAE, Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Ghana, Philippines, Ivory Coast.
- **Localization:** Multi-currency display, country selector, multi-lingual support, and region-specific delivery centers / DLCP stores.

### 1.4 Core Objective
Completely transform the legacy multi-page UI into a modern, lightning-fast, conversion-optimized, responsive web application inspired by contemporary e-commerce standards, with glassmorphic aesthetics, ultra-fluid micro-interactions, seamless mega-menus, and an integrated distributor hub.

## 4. Frontend & Design System Requirements

### 4.1 Aesthetic Direction
- **Primary Color Palette:** Deep Royal Sapphire (`#0a2540` / `#003366`), Emerald Wellness Green (`#10b981`), Vibrant Gold Accent (`#f59e0b` / `#d97706`), Warm Coral (`#f43f5e`), Clean Snow White & Slate Neutrals.
- **Visual Style:** Glassmorphic navigation headers, smooth backdrop filters (`blur(16px)`), modern rounded cards (`16px-24px`), subtle drop shadows, and high-contrast typography (Inter & Outfit).
- **Interactive Elements:** Smooth product carousels with drag/touch support, live filtering pills, instant modal quick views, animated PV counters, and responsive drawer cart.
- **Accessibility:** Full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, semantic HTML5 tags, keyboard navigation support, and zero layout shift.
